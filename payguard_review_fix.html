    Policy invariants
    1. No bank change via email alone.
    2. Fresh, trusted worker identity proof required.
    3. High-risk anomalies additionally require human review.
    4. Old payment instructions are not overwritten during pending review.
    5. Changes and decisions produce an auditable event.

    From detection to a coordinated response

    The underlying Reddit failure was both authentication and operations: a request succeeded without confirmation, and the employee discovered the problem after a missed payday. This playbook is a product workflow concept, not legal or compliance advice.

    1

    Contain the payout change

    Place pending, high-risk bank changes on hold. Preserve last verified instructions, flag upcoming payroll-cutoff exposure and prevent unsafe self-approval.

    2

    Verify through an existing trust anchor

    Ask the worker to independently open the known payroll application and complete phishing-resistant re-authentication. Never rely on replying to the initiating email.

    3

    Orchestrate the right response

    Fraud Ops owns case triage; Payroll/Treasury validates pay-run exposure; Security investigates account takeover; Legal/GRC advises as needed. Escalate suspected already-diverted wages promptly.

    4

    Close the loop and improve

    Inform the worker through trusted channels, preserve audit logs, establish disposition and track time-to-resolution, manual review rate, false positives and confirmed fraud losses.

    Where AI helps — and where it should not be the authority

    AI assists: cluster similar fraud narratives, draft a threat model, propose candidate risk rules, generate synthetic tests and summarize cases for analysts with provenance.

    Deterministic and human controls remain: enforce bank-change authorization, perform real identity verification, make high-stakes release decisions and keep an auditable record.

    Production follow-on: event stream from verified portal changes; IdP authentication events; device and session signals; payroll schedule; bank-token changes; anonymized confirmed case outcomes. Backtest and calibrate policies before rollout.
